Key Takeaways


  1. What is the Format and Structure of a Formal Letter?
    A formal letter follows a specific structure, including the sender's address on the left, date below it, receiver's address on the right, subject line, salutation, body paragraphs, complimentary close, and signature with full name at the end.

  2. How to Write the Introduction Paragraph?
    When writing the introduction, you must clearly state who you are and why you are writing.

  3. What is Included in the Main Body Paragraph?
    The main body is needed to provide a detailed explanation, relevant facts, specific dates and supporting reasons for your request.

  4. How to Write the Conclusion Paragraph?
    The conclusion is written at the end, and it clearly states what action you want the receiver to take.

  5. How to End a Formal Letter?
    To end a formal letter, you need to sign off properly and write your full name.

Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Exigency
Meaning: A situation that demands immediate attention or action; an urgent need or crisis.
Example: Due to the medical exigency, the surgeon had to operate without waiting for the full team to arrive.

Consanguine
Meaning: People who are related by blood; biological relatives. The term is often used in anthropology or legal contexts.
Example: The ancient tribe maintained strict marriage customs, allowing unions only between distant consanguines to preserve lineage purity.

Aver
Meaning: To assert or affirm with confidence; to declare something to be true.
Example: The witness averred that she saw the defendant at the scene of the crime just before midnight.
